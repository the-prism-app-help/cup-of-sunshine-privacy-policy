# Cup of Sunshine Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 12, 2026**

Cup of Sunshine ("the app") is a pour-over coffee guide for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. In short: **the app does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any of your information. Everything you add stays on your iPhone.**

## Information the app does not collect

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers are collected.
- No tracking of any kind, and no ads.
- The app does not connect to any server run by the developer.

## Information stored on your device

The things you add in the app are saved only on your iPhone:

- Your name and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
- Your coffee beans, recipes, favorites, brew history, ratings, and passport stamps.
- Your settings, such as text size, color, appearance, water heating, temperature units, voice, sound, and animation choices.

The developer cannot see or access this information. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this information may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

## Camera and photos

The app asks to use your camera so you can take a profile picture or a photo of a coffee bag. You can also choose a picture from your photo library. Photos you pick are saved only inside the app on your iPhone. The app does not upload them anywhere and does not read any other photos in your library.

## Image Playground

On iPhones that support it, after you add a profile photo the app can open Apple's Image Playground to give your photo a coffee-themed twist. Image Playground is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer does not receive your photo or the image it creates. The new image is saved only on your iPhone, and you can cancel at any time to keep your original photo.

## Spoken guide and Personal Voice

The app reads the brewing steps aloud using voices built into iOS. Speech is created on your iPhone. If you choose to use your Personal Voice, iOS asks for your permission first. Your Personal Voice is used only on your iPhone to speak the brewing steps and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere by the app. You can change this permission at any time in Settings, under Accessibility, then Personal Voice.

The app does not use the microphone.

## Sharing

If you choose to share or print a recipe, the app creates the recipe page and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app or service you choose.

## Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Features provided by Apple, such as Image Playground, iOS voices, and the share sheet, are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared.

## Deleting your information

You can delete beans, recipes, brews, and your photo at any time in the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it saved.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
