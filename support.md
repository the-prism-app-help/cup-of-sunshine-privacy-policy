# Cup of Sunshine Support

Thanks for brewing with Cup of Sunshine! If something isn't working, or you have an idea to make the app better, we'd love to hear from you.

- Email: [the.prism.app.help@gmail.com](mailto:the.prism.app.help@gmail.com)

## Common questions

**How do I make the words bigger?**
Open the Me tab and pick a larger text size. Everything in the app changes right away.

**How do I change the speaking voice?**
Open the Me tab, go to Speaking voice, and tap Change voice. To get a more natural voice, download a Premium or Enhanced English voice in Settings, under Accessibility, then Read & Speak, then Voices. It shows up in the app once it finishes downloading.

**How do I turn the voice off while brewing?**
Tap the speaker button at the top right of the pouring screen.

**Is my information private?**
Yes. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the [privacy policy](./).
